Gillett

town, 1894 on

A Midland Terminal Railway town in Teller County, Colorado, platted in 1894 on the flats north-east of Cripple Creek. It peaked about 1896 to 1897, held the bullfight of August 1895 in a timber ring inside its racetrack, and faded after 1900. Nothing stands today but the ruin of the Catholic church.

How well it is known

Every position in the atlas carries a grade and an error. Here: 291 structures are measured, 9 inferred from measured things, and 122 are labelled conjecture. 53 photographs are shown, 25 more are listed but held elsewhere; 14 camera stations are placed, 10 of them solved.

How this began

This reconstruction began as a response to the photographs of Gillett shared by the Victor Heritage Society. Their photographs are not reproduced here unless their holders have cleared them; each is credited where it is used.
