Pikes Peak Rail Atlas

The railways into the Cripple Creek gold district and the places along them, as they stood in any year from 1887 to 1949.

Every stretch of line and every place says how it is known: measured, inferred or conjecture, to how many feet, and from what source.

  1. Pick a year, or one of the marked years.
  2. Click a line or a place on the map to see how it is known.
  3. Open one of the eight places worked up in full, below.

Phantom Canyon, 1894 and 1896. The Florence and Cripple Creek's first line on the canyon floor, wrecked by the cloudburst of 30 July 1895, and the 'high line' that replaced it on 27 June 1896 are now both drawn, each in its years; the northern tunnel belongs to the second. From newspapers of the time and the lidar. Use the 1895 and 1896 buttons under the year.

Sixteen of that railway's stations pinned by the stationing of its own survey of 1894, from Florence to Anaconda.

Cripple Creek to Victor and the Arkansas crossing at Florence redrawn from the same survey: 5.67 miles depot to depot against the timetables' 5.7.
